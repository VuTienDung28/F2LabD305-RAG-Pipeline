Alumni Card – Rules and Regulations

1.  Eligibility

1.1 When applying for Alumni card, proof of status will be required. You have to prove
that you  ’ve completed degree programs at the time you apply for alumni card. An
alumni card will only be granted when appropriate proof of status is presented.

1.2 RMIT Vietnam’s alumni are also subject to Regulations for Conduct and Rules for
the  use  of  the  University  Sports  Facilities  and  University  Library  found  in  the
University Calendar.

2.  Use of Alumni card

2.1 All alumni must present their Alumni card to gain access to the RMIT University
facilities such as REC and library. Failure to present your Alumni card upon arrival
may result in a refusal of access to the facilities.

2.2 Alumni card does not always guarantee instant usage to facilities

2.3 There is no expiration date for alumni card no matter when you graduated.

2.4. You can request to reprint your alumni card in case of loss or breakage with no
fee charge. However, you cannot do so within the first year of your first card request.

2.5 Alumni card is non-transferable: should another individual use your card to attempt
to  gain  access  to  the  facility, RMIT  University  reserves  the  right  to  terminate  your
Alumni card

3. Use of RMIT Vietnam’s Facilities

3.1 All Alumni are entitled to use the facilities which are accessible by Alumni during
opening hours. RMIT Vietnam does, however, reserve the right to close the facility for
any period of time when required for tournaments, exhibitions or other activities, or in
connection with any repair, alteration or other maintenance work. Advance notice of
any closure periods will be given wherever possible.

3.3 RMIT Vietnam only grants alumni access to the facilities. All alumni who wish to
join in any sport classes must follow the classes’ regulations.

3.4 RMIT Vietnam reserves the right to refuse entry and use of the facility should an
individual behaviour or appearance be deemed inappropriate.

3.5 Children under the age of 13 are not permitted to enter the indoor facilities unless
they are to be supervised by a responsible person whilst taking part in any on-campus
activities

3.6 Lost or stolen property should be reported to reception as soon as possible where
the matter will be dealt with in the appropriate manner.

3.7 Notices and signs displayed around RMIT Vietnam facilities (indoor and outdoor)
must be obeyed at all times. Notice boards will be updated with important information
and should be regularly checked by all Alumni.

3.8  All  accidents  that  occur  on  RMIT  Vietnam  facilities  must  be  reported  to  the
Security immediately by contacting reception.

3.9 RMIT Vietnam’s liability for loss or damage to your property is strictly limited to
any  loss  or  damage  suffered  as  a  result  of  our  negligence  or  our  failure  to  take
reasonable care. With this exception we will not accept liability for the safety of your
personal  property  brought  onto  the  premises.  Any  property  stored  in  the  lockers  is
stored at the individual risk and no liability will be accepted by the RMIT Vietnam.
Any vehicles parked in RMIT Vietnam parking areas, including all contents, are the
owner’s responsibility and no liability will be accepted by the RMIT Vietnam.
