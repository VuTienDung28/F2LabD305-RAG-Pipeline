RMIT Vietnam Library is delighted to welcome students visiting the library on Experience Day, the biggest event for high school students and parents to experience what life at RMIT is really like.

Here are some highlights from two amazing Sundays at both Saigon South and Hanoi campuses:

Over 6.000 visitors in total to the library and library booth.

Hands-on experience with cutting-edge library services and facilities.

Discovering a wide range of books and resources available exclusively at RMIT Vietnam.

Fun games and exciting prizes at Library booth that brought big smiles all around.

Library tour at Hanoi campus during Experience Day 2025

High school students learning about RMIT Experience Day

In celebration of RMIT University’s 25 years in Vietnam, this year’s Experience Day was our biggest ever, with 100+ trial classes, 40+ student clubs, 35+ info sessions, 25+ exhibitions, meet-and-greets with students, alumni, parents, staff, academics and live performances.

A special thank to nearly 10,000 students and parents for making Experience Day 2025 an unforgettable blast in both Saigon South campus and Hanoi campus.

Happy Holidays, from the Library, and see you at more events in 2026.
