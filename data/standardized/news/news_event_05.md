# "Words That Inspire" writing contest awards ceremony: celebrating stories across campuses

**Source:** https://www.rmit.edu.vn/libraryvn/about-us/news/2025/words-that-inspire-recap
**Crawled:** 2026-08-04T03:28:38.321711+00:00

---

The "Words That Inspire" writing contest has ended with heartfelt moments and meaningful reflections, celebrating the power of learning and the role of the library in personal journeys across the RMIT Vietnam community.

The awards ceremonies took place on 10 December at Saigon South campus and 12 December at Hanoi campus. Across both campuses, the ceremonies created a warm and reflective atmosphere where outstanding submissions were recognised and stories were celebrated.

One of the contesters sharing experience at Saigon South campus

"The events offered an opportunity for the community to pause, listen, and reflect on the importance of storytelling in education and the library’s role as a companion in learning and development."

“The library had the book I couldn’t find anywhere else. It was a small moment, but it made me feel supported and understood, like someone had thought of exactly what I needed.” - sharing from a participant.

Sharing from one of Hanoi campus’ winners

Winners across campuses

Grand winners: Tran Thuy Hoang Uyen (Saigon South campus), Nguyen Danh Khanh Phuc (Hanoi campus)

Second place: Pham Thanh Thao (Saigon South campus), Bui Nhat Minh (Hanoi campus)

People’s choice: Trinh Quang Thien (Saigon South campus), Hoang Huyen Thanh (Hanoi campus)

Grand winner - Tran Hoang Thuy Uyen

These ceremonies not only honoured exceptional stories but also created meaningful moments of connection and reflection across the RMIT Vietnam community. The RMIT Vietnam Library sincerely thanks all participants and contributors for sharing their stories and making Words That Inspire contest a truly inspiring experience.
