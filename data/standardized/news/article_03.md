# The library welcomes visitors from Can Tho University

**Source:** https://www.rmit.edu.vn/libraryvn/about-us/news/2025/library-welcomes-visitors-from-can-tho-university  
**Crawled:** 2026-08-04T03:28:37.440812+00:00

---

On Friday, 20 March, RMIT Vietnam Library had the privilege of welcoming more than 60 students and staff from Can Tho University, majoring in Library and Information Studies, to the Saigon South campus.

The visit served as a valuable field trip, allowing students to observe and gain practical insights into how a professional academic library operates. Colleagues at the Beanland Library delivered a detailed introduction to the library services and support available to the RMIT community. The visit concluded with a library tour, where participants experienced a glimpse of the daily operations and vibrant atmosphere of RMIT Vietnam Library.

This meaningful cross-university exchange highlighted the strengths and values of RMIT, particularly in fostering inclusivity, engagement and strategic partnerships that contribute to a thriving library community in Vietnam.
